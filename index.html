03 — Safety

A 360° camera on every helper.

During every cleaning the helper wears a 360° body camera. It protects your belongings from loss and theft — and protects the helper from false claims.

  • 01

    Recording from start to finish

    The camera turns on at your door and turns off when the helper leaves.

  • 02

    Private and encrypted

    Footage is stored encrypted for 30 days and opened only if there is a claim — by you or by us.

  • 03

    Loss and damage cover

    If something is lost or broken during the order, HOMIE investigates and compensates.

  • 04

    Verified identity

    Every helper passes an ID check, experience check and personal interview.
